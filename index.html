Our factory · AQJ Apparels, Tamil Nadu, India

We don't just sell it. We make it.

Behind Five Threads stands our own factory, AQJ Apparels. From yarn to the packed carton, our clothes are knitted, cut, printed, stitched and checked under one roof.

AQJ has been crafting garments for children, men and beyond since 2014, for domestic and international brands. That is why a design number you reorder from our shop comes back the same, season after season.
